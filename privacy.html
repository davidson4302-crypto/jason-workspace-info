Privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026

This policy describes Jason Workspace, a privately distributed Mac application maintained by Jason Davidson. Contact davidson4302@gmail.com about privacy or account access.

Google account access

You connect each account using Google OAuth. The app receives authorization tokens, not your Google password. With your permission, it accesses Gmail messages, sender and recipient information, message bodies, labels, and attachments so you can read, search, organize, and reply to mail. It accesses your calendar list and calendar events to display your schedule and create events, including Google Meet conference links.

Email sends happen when you submit Send. Calendar invitations happen when you submit an event with attendees. Starting an immediate meeting opens Google Meet. The app does not require access to your Google account password.

Optional AI and HubSpot features

When you request an inbox review, or enable automatic review, the app sends selected email content, including sender, recipient, subject, and message text, together with your selected instructions and preferences, to your active provider: OpenAI or xAI (Grok). Only the selected provider receives that request. AI results can include summaries, suggested tasks, and draft replies. Nothing is emailed automatically.

AI processing occurs in your provider’s cloud under your connected account. The provider’s terms and account privacy controls govern its processing and retention. Review those controls, including model-improvement settings, before enabling email review. The app does not control or promise a particular retention period for your provider account.

If you connect HubSpot, the app accesses the owner information needed to select your tasks and reads and updates your linked task titles, notes, priorities, due dates, and completion status. Saving an email-derived task to HubSpot shares the task information you submit with HubSpot.

Storage and security

Google and HubSpot tokens, settings, tasks, uploaded text skills, and assistant results are stored in an encrypted workspace file on the Mac running the app. The encryption key is stored on that same Mac with file-access restrictions. The Mac owner must protect the user account, disk, network access, and backups. Connected AI clients also maintain their own local account and session files.

The app fetches email and calendar content for display. It does not save a complete local mailbox. Assistant results may contain email-derived text and remain in the workspace until removed. Downloaded attachments, provider session files, and Mac backups may retain copies separately.

Sharing and images

Workspace data is not sent to a central Jason Workspace hosting service. It is exchanged with Google, your selected AI provider when used, and HubSpot when connected. Remote images in opened emails load from their original hosts without a referrer; those hosts can receive your IP address and may record an image view. Embedded email images are fetched through your connected Gmail account.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train our own general-purpose AI models. Google user data is used to provide the workspace features described here. Its use and transfer are subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and retention

You can disable automatic review, switch the AI provider, disconnect an integration, or revoke Google access through your Google Account’s third-party connection settings. Disconnecting Google removes that account’s active authorization tokens from the workspace, but does not automatically erase earlier assistant results, previous encrypted workspace copies, downloaded files, or backups.

Workspace data has no automatic expiration. To remove all local workspace data, stop the app and remove its application-support data and any related provider files and backups you control. Contact the maintainer for help identifying the files. Disconnecting the app does not delete your original Gmail messages, Google calendars, or HubSpot records.

This public website

This informational website has no sign-in, advertising, analytics scripts, or contact form. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process standard web-request information to operate and secure the site, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The workspace uses a session cookie to keep you signed in to your local app.

Policy updates

Material changes to data handling will be reflected here with a new effective date. Contact the maintainer if you need clarification before connecting an account.
