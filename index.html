A personal workspace for Mac

Your inbox, your tasks,
your day. Together.

Jason Workspace brings your Gmail accounts, Google calendars, meetings, and next steps into one private dashboard running on your Mac.

A unified Gmail inbox

Connect up to ten Google accounts. Read formatted messages, search mail, star or archive emails, and compose replies from one place.

Your calendars and Google Meet

See daily and weekly schedules across connected Google calendars. Create calendar events with Meet links, or open Google Meet to start a meeting.

A task board that stays with you

Organize work into To do, In progress, and Done. Optionally connect HubSpot to sync tasks assigned to you.

An optional inbox assistant

Choose OpenAI or Grok to summarize selected emails, suggest tasks, and prepare reply drafts. Customize instructions with your own text skills. Review and send replies yourself.

One workspace on your two Macs

Run the workspace on your Mac mini and access that same workspace from your MacBook. Google sign-in happens on Google’s authorization page, where you choose the accounts and permissions to grant.

Workspace data is stored on the Mac running the app. Optional assistant features send selected email content to your chosen AI provider. Read the privacy policy for the full data flow.
